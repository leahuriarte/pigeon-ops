      Is a drone check worth the wait?

      PigeonOps is a decision aid for a port dispatcher. When a road report comes in, it works out whether checking the road with a drone is worth the cost and the delay, then ranks the possible responses. The dispatcher makes every call.

      1. An incident report arrives. A road on a critical delivery’s route may be blocked.
      2. You decide whether to check by drone. You see what the check costs and what it is expected to save.
      3. You choose a response. Keep the route, reroute, or swap trucks.
      4. You watch it play out. Cost, delay and CO₂ add up on the map, then a shift report.
